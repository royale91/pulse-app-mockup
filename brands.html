The problem

Emerging brands fight three losing battles every season.

Big houses have WGSN, McKinsey reports, and full studios. You have a Notion doc, a stylist DM thread, and 14 browser tabs of "what's trending right now." That gap is what pulse Brands closes.

i.

Trend forecasting is gated

WGSN, Trendalytics, Edited — all $20–50K/yr. Pinterest analytics are vague. TikTok signals are noisy. You guess, then you over-produce dead inventory.

$50K/yr · or you guess
ii.

Line sheets eat seasons alive

Every drop, someone spends 8–16 hours in Photoshop manually building a buyer line sheet. SKUs, colorways, WSP, MOQ. Buyer wants a tweak — start over.

~$1,200 / drop · 4–8 drops/yr
iii.

Ad ops is duplicated work

Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, your website. Same products, four sets of creative, four dashboards, four pixel setups. Your agency charges $5K/mo to do this badly.

$60K/yr · agency + waste
